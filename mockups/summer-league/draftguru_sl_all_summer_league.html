Selection Methodology
How DraftGuru picks All-Summer-League — fixed rules, no editorial discretion
The Rules
  • Position-balanced teams. Each team is 2 guards + 2 forwards + 1 center. Positions sourced from PlayerMaster.position (final NBA roster designation, not pre-draft scouting tag).
  • Pure stats composite. Selection within each position slot is by SL composite score TBD — no team-success bonus, no narrative bias, no "name brand" tiebreaker.
  • Sample floor. Player must have at least 60 total MIN + 2 GP in the season to qualify (same threshold as leaderboards). Injured-list players who never tipped (e.g., Topić '24) are excluded.
  • No team-success multiplier. The Vegas champion doesn't get a bump. Composite is what plays. (This is why a player on a non-bracket team can still make 1st team.)
  • Combo-G / PF-C tiebreakers. Players eligible for two positions TBD are assigned to whichever slot maximizes the team's total composite — i.e., the algorithm fits them where the position is weakest.
  • Sub-awards. MVP = #1 composite, regardless of position. Best Rookie = first-time SL player with the highest composite (MVP excluded if first SL). Most Improved = largest YoY composite jump (≥3 GP each year). Defensive Player = highest per-36 stocks + best on-court DRtg swing, ≥80 MIN.
  • Editorial scope. DraftGuru does not vote, edit, or override the selections. The page regenerates nightly during SL; once a year's SL is complete, the page locks.
