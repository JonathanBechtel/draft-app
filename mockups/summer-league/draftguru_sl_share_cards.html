Summer League Share Cards

Six SVG → PNG variants surfaced by the SL section. Every public SL page has a Share as PNG button that routes to the matching template. Cards below render at half scale; production output is 1200×630.

Output format
1200 × 630 PNG
Twitter / OG / Facebook compatible
Render path
SVG → PNG (server-side)
Headless render at request time, cached 24h
Typography
Russo One + Azeret Mono
Embedded as WOFF2 in the SVG
Treatment
Dark slate · scanlines · pixel corners · amber accent
Per docs/style_guide.md · matches dark surfaces in app
