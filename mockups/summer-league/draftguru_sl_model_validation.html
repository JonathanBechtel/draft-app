What this reveals: the consensus board is highly predictive at the top (top-5 hit rate 80%) and noisiest in mid-lotto (picks 6–14, just 44%). R2 is where consensus is most pessimistic — the average R2 player slightly outperforms their slot, suggesting consensus boards systematically over-discount this tier.
