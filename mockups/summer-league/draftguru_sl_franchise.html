Lakers SL Storylines
The patterns across 22 years of Lakers Summer League rosters
Franchise peak 2017 Vegas
The Lonzo / Kuzma summer was a once-in-a-decade SL run
6-0 record, the Vegas title, Ball as MVP, and Kuzma on the 1st team. Largest single-year delta over franchise SL baseline (+0.40 win%) — and both players cleared a 70 composite, the only Lakers duo to do that in the same SL.
NBA hit rate SL → rotation
68% of Lakers SL stars carved out NBA roles
Of 19 Lakers SL players with a composite ≥65, 13 became NBA rotation players or better. Above the league SL→NBA conversion rate (52% for that composite tier post-2015) — Lakers SL produces real signal.
Franchise miss 2014–15 era
Worst SL run came right before the rebuild took off
Combined 4-12 across 2014–2015 Vegas. Julius Randle and D'Angelo Russell both posted sub-50 composites in their first SLs — both eventually traded. SL doesn't always project; this era is the cautionary case.
